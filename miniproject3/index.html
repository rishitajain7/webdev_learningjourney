Our Story

Built around curiosity.

Greenwood Public Library began as a small neighborhood reading room and has grown into a vibrant community destination.

Today, visitors can browse thousands of books, access digital resources, attend workshops, or simply find a quiet place to think.

We believe libraries should be accessible, inspiring, and welcoming to everyone.

Whether you are looking for your next great story or learning something completely new, there is a place for you here.
